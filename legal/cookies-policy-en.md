# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** May 2, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how MARCALL ("we") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit marcall.careofaddress.com or use the platform. It complements the [Privacy Notice](/legal/privacy).

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser to remember information. We use them alongside similar technologies such as local storage (`localStorage`, `sessionStorage`) and anonymous browser identifiers.

## 2. Categories of cookies we use

### 2.1 Essential (always active)

These cookies are necessary for the site to function and cannot be disabled. Without them, you cannot sign in or protect your account.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `__Host-marcall_sid` | Authenticated session | Session / 30 days |
| `__Host-marcall_csrf` | CSRF protection | Session |
| `__Host-marcall_consent` | Remembers your cookie preferences | 1 year |

### 2.2 Analytics (opt-in, requires your consent)

These cookies help us understand how the site is used, without identifying you personally. They are activated **only if you accept them** from the cookie banner.

| Provider | Purpose | Duration | Policy |
|----------|---------|----------|--------|
| Plausible Analytics | Anonymous usage analytics | No persistent cookies (daily fingerprint) | [plausible.io/privacy](https://plausible.io/privacy) |

Plausible is a cookie-less analytics provider that does not track across websites or build persistent profiles.

### 2.3 We do not use

- Advertising cookies
- Social network cookies (no Meta, Google Ads, or TikTok pixels)
- Third-party cross-site tracking cookies

## 3. Managing your preferences

- **On MARCALL:** use the cookie banner shown on your first visit. You can accept all, accept essential only, or dismiss the banner (equivalent to essential only).
- **In your browser:** you can delete or block cookies from your browser's privacy settings. This may affect site functionality (for example, you will not be able to stay signed in).

## 4. Changes to this policy

If we expand the categories of cookies (for example, by adding a customer support provider), we will update this page and, where applicable, ask for additional consent.

## 5. Contact

If you have questions about cookies or about the processing of your personal data, contact us at **privacy@careofaddress.com** or see the [full Privacy Notice](/legal/privacy).
