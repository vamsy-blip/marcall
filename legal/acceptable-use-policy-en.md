# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Last updated:** May 2, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy is incorporated by reference into the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and applies to all MARCALL users.

## 1. Prohibited uses

You will not use MARCALL to:

1. **Illegal activity** under the laws of Mexico, the United States, or any applicable jurisdiction.
2. **Unsolicited calls (voice spam, robocalls):** dialing numbers you are not authorized to contact, violating do-not-call lists (e.g. Mexico's Repep, the U.S. National Do Not Call Registry), or circumventing prior-consent requirements.
3. **Caller-ID spoofing:** transmitting false or misleading origination numbers in violation of FCC, IFT, or other applicable rules.
4. **Fraud:** phishing, vishing, smishing, advance-fee schemes, brand impersonation, or any attempt to obtain payment information or credentials by deceiving the call recipient.
5. **Harassment, threats, or intimidation** of callers, prospects, or third parties.
6. **Prohibited content:** material promoting child sexual abuse, violence against persons, terrorism, or exploitation of minors.
7. **Intellectual-property violations:** cloning third-party voices without authorization, reproducing copyrighted audio, or infringing trademarks.
8. **Technical abuse:** attempting to compromise MARCALL's infrastructure, its integrations, or other customers' systems; reverse-engineering AI models; sending automated fraudulent traffic.
9. **Sensitive data out of scope:** processing HIPAA-regulated health data, COPPA-regulated minor data, or classified government information without a specific written agreement authorizing it.
10. **Unauthorized reselling:** reselling the service without enrolling in the [Reseller Program](/legal/resellers).

## 2. Fair use

MARCALL enforces reasonable usage limits per plan (call volume, duration, concurrency). Usage that significantly exceeds category averages may be audited and, if determined abusive, throttled or terminated.

## 3. Caller consent

You are solely responsible for obtaining any consent required from callers to:

- Record the call (MARCALL announces this automatically at the start of every call).
- Process the voice with AI and transcribe the content.
- Store caller metadata (number, time, duration, transcript).

MARCALL provides a notice announcement at the start of each call, but substantive compliance with applicable law (LFPDPPP, GDPR, TCPA) rests with you.

## 4. Reporting abuse

If you received a suspicious call originated from MARCALL, report it to **abuse@careofaddress.com** with:
- The number you were called from
- The approximate date and time
- A summary of the content

We investigate all abuse reports within 72 business hours.

## 5. Consequences of non-compliance

Violations of this policy may result in (a) warning, (b) immediate service suspension without refund, (c) account termination, and (d) reporting to authorities where applicable. For severe violations (fraud, illegal content), MARCALL may act without prior notice.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified at least 14 days in advance via the registered email and a notice in the customer dashboard.
