# Reseller Program (Plan Agencia) Terms

Available in [Español](/legal/resellers?lang=es) | English

**Last updated:** May 2, 2026
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Preamble

These Reseller Program Terms (the **"Program Terms"**) apply exclusively to Customers who subscribe to MARCALL's **Agencia Plan** and wish to operate as authorized Resellers. These Program Terms supplement and are incorporated into MARCALL's Terms of Service (the **"General Terms"**). In the event of a conflict between the two documents, these Program Terms shall govern with respect to the Reseller Program.

---

## 1. Program-Specific Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Reseller** | The Customer under the Agencia Plan who has accepted these Program Terms and is authorized to create and manage Sub-Tenants on the MARCALL platform |
| **Agency** | Synonym for Reseller; the individual or entity that provides consulting, marketing, technology, or related services and incorporates MARCALL into its service offering |
| **Sub-Tenant** | The tenant (MARCALL service instance) created by the Reseller on behalf of one of its end clients, which may operate under the Reseller's own brand or the MARCALL brand |
| **End Client** | The business or individual to whom the Reseller provides MARCALL services, whether under the Reseller's own brand, co-branded, or the MARCALL brand |
| **Agency Panel** | The administration interface within the MARCALL platform that allows the Reseller to create, manage, bill, and monitor Sub-Tenants |
| **Revenue Share** | The economic participation that MARCALL pays to the Reseller for active Sub-Tenants under the Reseller's account |
| **Stripe Connect** | The payment platform used to disburse Revenue Share payments to the Reseller |

## 2. Admission to the Program

2.1 To participate in the Program, the Customer must:
- Subscribe to and maintain an active **Agencia Plan** subscription.
- Provide complete and accurate tax information (RFC, legal business name, registered tax address) required for CFDI issuance and Stripe Connect onboarding.
- Complete **Stripe Connect** account setup through the Agency Panel, which is required in order to receive Revenue Share payments.
- Expressly accept these Program Terms via the mechanism provided in the Agency Panel.

2.2 MARCALL reserves the right to approve or reject any applicant's participation in the Program, at its sole discretion and without being required to state a reason.

## 3. Reseller's Responsibility for End Clients

3.1 The Reseller is **solely responsible**, vis-à-vis MARCALL and third parties, for:

a) Each End Client's compliance with MARCALL's General Terms.
b) Compliance with all applicable law in the activities conducted by End Clients through Sub-Tenants (including recording disclosures, compliance with NOM-184-SCFI-2018, the LFPDPPP, and all other applicable regulations).
c) Proper management of caller consents on behalf of End Clients.
d) Acts and omissions by End Clients that constitute violations of the Acceptable Use provisions of the General Terms.
e) Amounts owed in connection with inactive Sub-Tenants or outstanding billing obligations.

3.2 The Reseller must enter into a separate services agreement with each End Client that incorporates, at minimum, the Acceptable Use obligations set out in MARCALL's General Terms.

3.3 MARCALL has no direct contractual relationship with the Reseller's End Clients, unless those End Clients independently subscribe to MARCALL's service directly.

## 4. Revenue Share and Commissions

### 4.1 Percentage

The Reseller is entitled to receive a **25% Revenue Share** on net revenues (excluding VAT and overage Minutes charges) actually collected by MARCALL from each active Sub-Tenant under the Reseller's account, for the paid Plans subscribed to by those Sub-Tenants.

MARCALL may offer differentiated percentages or additional bonuses to high-performing Resellers, at its discretion and by written agreement.

### 4.2 Calculation and Settlement

- Revenue Share is calculated monthly, based on collections from the preceding calendar month.
- Settlement is made **within the first 10 business days** of the month following the accrual period.
- Payment is made through **Stripe Connect** to the bank account registered by the Reseller.
- The minimum payout threshold is **$500 MXN** per period. If the accrued amount is below that threshold, it carries forward to the following period until the minimum is reached.

### 4.3 Chargebacks and Refunds

If a Sub-Tenant receives a refund authorized by MARCALL under the General Terms, the corresponding Revenue Share will be deducted from the Reseller's next payment or charged to the Reseller's account if no sufficient balance exists.

### 4.4 Taxes on Revenue Share

Revenue Share constitutes taxable income for the Reseller. Each party is responsible for its own tax compliance obligations. MARCALL will issue the corresponding CFDI for Revenue Share payments where required under applicable Mexican tax law.

## 5. Branding and Service Presentation

### 5.1 Brand License

MARCALL grants the Reseller a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use the **"MARCALL"** name, its logos, and its authorized marketing materials solely to promote and sell the service to End Clients, subject to MARCALL's Brand Guidelines available at marcall.careofaddress.com/brand.

### 5.2 White-Label (Own-Brand) Presentation

The Agencia Plan includes the option to present the service under the **Reseller's own brand** (white-label), which includes:
- Use of the Reseller's custom domain on the Sub-Tenant administration portal.
- Use of the Reseller's chosen assistant name (for example, "Valeria from [Agency]" rather than "Sofía from MARCALL").
- Suppression of visible "MARCALL" references in the end-client interface, per available configuration options.

White-label authorization does not permit the Reseller to register trademarks incorporating "MARCALL" or to mislead anyone regarding the ownership of the underlying technology.

### 5.3 Prohibited Use of Brand Assets

The Reseller may not use MARCALL's brand assets in any way that: (i) causes confusion; (ii) damages MARCALL's reputation; (iii) implies an unauthorized association or endorsement; or (iv) violates the Brand Guidelines.

## 6. Limited Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation

6.1 **Non-Compete.** During the term of the Program, the Reseller agrees not to develop, market, or distribute, directly or indirectly, a product or service of its own that substantially replicates the core functionality of MARCALL (AI voice assistant for business call reception) and that is based, in whole or in part, on access to code, architecture, data, or trade secrets obtained through participation in the Program.

This restriction is limited to the territory of the **United Mexican States** and does not prevent the Reseller from integrating or reselling other technology or communications services.

6.2 **Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the term of the Program and for **12 months** after its termination, the Reseller agrees not to hire or actively solicit key MARCALL personnel (engineers, designers, executives) who had direct contact with the Reseller in connection with the Program.

6.3 **Non-Solicitation of Clients.** During the term of the Program, the Reseller agrees not to actively solicit MARCALL's direct customers to leave the platform in favor of competing services offered by the Reseller.

## 7. Payments to Reseller — Additional Conditions

7.1 The Reseller must maintain an active and current Stripe Connect account. MARCALL is not responsible for payment delays caused by incorrect banking information or suspended Stripe Connect accounts.

7.2 MARCALL may withhold Revenue Share payments if there is an active dispute or if the Reseller is in breach of these Program Terms, until the matter is resolved.

7.3 Upon termination of the Program, Revenue Share accrued through the termination date will be settled in the next regular payment cycle, net of any amounts owed by the Reseller to MARCALL.

## 8. Program Termination and Effects on Sub-Tenants

### 8.1 Grounds for Termination

The Program may be terminated:
- By the Reseller at any time, by canceling the Agencia Plan subscription as described in Section 11 of the General Terms.
- By MARCALL, with **30 calendar days'** prior written notice for cause (repeated non-compliance, fraud, reputational harm) or with **90 calendar days'** prior written notice without cause.
- Immediately by MARCALL if the Reseller engages in willful misconduct, fraud, material violations of applicable law, or activities that put MARCALL or End Clients at risk.

### 8.2 Effects on Sub-Tenants

Upon termination of the Program:

a) **Migration option.** Active Sub-Tenants may migrate directly to the MARCALL platform under current standard plans, if MARCALL makes this option available, with their existing configuration preserved to the extent possible.

b) **Churn option.** If a Sub-Tenant does not wish to continue with MARCALL directly, service will be suspended at the end of the Sub-Tenant's current billing period.

c) **Reseller's responsibility.** The Reseller is responsible for notifying its End Clients of the Program's termination with sufficient advance notice (at minimum, the same notice period received from MARCALL) to avoid unplanned service interruptions.

d) **Data.** Sub-Tenant data (recordings, configurations) is subject to MARCALL's Data Retention Policy and the data export rights set out in the General Terms.
