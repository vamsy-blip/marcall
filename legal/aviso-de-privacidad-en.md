## 5. ARCO Rights and How to Exercise Them

As a data subject, you have the following rights under the LFPDPPP (collectively, "ARCO rights"):

You may exercise your ARCO rights directly from your account panel at **marcall.careofaddress.com/app** or by email to **privacy@careofaddress.com**.

- **Access:** Know what personal data we hold about you, the purposes for which it is processed, and the conditions of that processing.
- **Rectification:** Request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Cancellation:** Request the deletion of your data when it is no longer necessary for the purpose that justified its collection, or when required by law.
- **Objection:** Object to the processing of your data for specific purposes.

### 5.1 Procedure for Exercising ARCO Rights

To exercise any of these rights, you or your duly authorized legal representative must send a request to **privacidad@careofaddress.com** with the subject line: **"ARCO Rights Request"**, including:

1. Your full name as the data subject.
2. The email address registered on your account, or a government-issued photo ID (and, if acting through a representative, a notarized power of attorney).
3. A clear and specific description of the right you wish to exercise and of the personal data to which the request relates.
4. Any documents or information that may help locate the relevant data.

**Response timelines:**
- MARCALL will acknowledge receipt of your request within **2 business days** of receiving it.
- A resolution will be issued within **20 business days** of the date on which a complete request is received.
- Where the request is granted, the applicable changes will take effect within the following **15 business days**.

These timelines may be extended on a single occasion for an equal additional period, with prior notice to you, in accordance with Article 32 of the LFPDPPP.
